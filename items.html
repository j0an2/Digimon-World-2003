Items

Every item, what it really does, and where to get it

What each item does, read from the game's code (European version), not only from its description. Click a row to see who drops it. Drop % is the chance in a battle won against that enemy (one item per battle at most, from one of the enemies). The same item is the one you can steal.

How equipment works

Slots

Each partner wears up to two hands (a weapon, a second weapon or a shield; two-handed weapons take both), head, body and two accessories. The two accessories can't be from the same group: one Fire Power and one Water Power can't go together (both are element powers), nor a Dance Feather with a Dance Wing. Gems and rings have no group, so two Power Rings can.

Weapons

  • Strength adds to the Attack of physical hits; Accuracy adds to your Speed when the game rolls whether a physical hit lands (see Hit chance).
  • Family weapons (Trent, Angel Bow, Excalibur, the Dramon and legendary ones…): ×1.5 damage and +16/128 critical chance against that family.
  • Status weapons (Venom Fang, Raven Bow, Rock Breaker, Needle Glove, Soul Chopper) and drain weapons (Crimson Blade, Cerberus Fang, Vampire Lance): the plain attack may inflict their effect. The Shock Horn never paralyzes: its chance is 0 (see Bugs).
  • Saber Fang and Shuriken raise the critical chance like the Search Crest. While a status, drain or critical weapon is worn, the Multi Crest, Search Crest and Counter Crest do nothing.

Armor

Defense, sometimes an element resistance, and Evasion: it counts as that much more Speed only when an enemy rolls whether its physical hit lands (see Evasion). Every 8 points are about 1% less chance of being hit; it does nothing against magic.

Accessories

  • Gems and rings: a flat stat or element resistance. The status rings (Antidote, Revive, Awake, Sober, Prayer) replace the Digimon's own resistance with 40, so they only help when that is lower.
  • Element powers (Fire Power…): attacks without an element of their own get the accessory's element, like a technique's (see the example).
  • Search Crest: +32/128 critical chance for techniques with no effect, more for Busters (see Critical hits), but those same techniques lose a quarter of their accuracy (a half with the Saber Fang): a basic attack of accuracy 110 goes from 89% to 67%, unless you are far above the enemy. Multi Crest: three hits of 40% (1.2× in all, each hit rolled) for every physical technique with no effect of its own, the basic attack and the likes of Mechanical Bash; a technique that already hits several times (Hammer Rush) keeps its own hits.
  • HP Proxy: a fixed amount off every hit taken. MP Proxy: techniques cost less MP.
  • Recover Crest: some HP back every turn of a battle. EXP / BIT Adapter: +20% after battles.
  • Glasses, Goggles: more accuracy. Dance Feather, Dance Wing: a little evasion, hardly noticeable.
  • Training Book, Power Brace: the gym's bonus try works a little more often.

Usable items

  • Chips raise a stat for good by a random amount (1 to 10; 1 to 30 for max HP and MP), never above 999 (9999 for HP and MP). Train Chips give TP for the gym.
  • Disks, Plugs and Fields are for battles. Plug boosts and the enemy debuffs wear off after a few turns.
